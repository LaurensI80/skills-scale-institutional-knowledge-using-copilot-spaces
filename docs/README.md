# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation. This README serves as your central entry point for understanding how OctoAcme runs projects and where to find guidance for each phase of delivery.

## Quick Start

OctoAcme follows a **phased, customer-first approach** to project delivery with clear roles, iterative delivery, and data-informed decisions. Our framework is designed to:

- Deliver customer value through small, testable increments
- Maintain clear ownership and accountability across projects
- Make decisions based on evidence and metrics
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning

## Project Lifecycle Overview

OctoAcme projects follow a five-phase lifecycle that balances planning rigor with execution agility:

### 1. **Initiation** – Validate & Authorize
Confirm business need and measurable outcome, identify stakeholders and champions, define success criteria and initial timeline. This phase culminates in a lightweight Project One-pager and a go/no-go decision to move into planning.

### 2. **Planning** – Break Down & Align
Turn the approved initiative into an actionable backlog and milestone plan. Identify dependencies, integrate across teams, define quality standards, and estimate effort. The team emerges with clear scope, responsibilities, and release expectations.

### 3. **Execution** – Build, Test & Iterate
Deliver work in small increments with daily standups, regular demos, and continuous quality checks. Team members collaborate on design and testability, participate in code reviews, and help identify technical risks. Communication is frequent and escalation paths are clear.

### 4. **Release** – Deploy with Confidence
Move validated features to production through a standardized process. Pre-release checklists ensure all acceptance criteria are met, tests pass, security scanning is complete, and rollback plans are documented. Post-deploy verification confirms the release is healthy.

### 5. **Close & Retrospective** – Capture Learnings
Conduct a retrospective to review what went well, what could improve, and which actions should be tracked. Learnings are converted into actionable improvements and feedback is incorporated into future projects.

## Key Artifacts & Templates

Throughout the project lifecycle, OctoAcme uses several key artifacts to maintain alignment and capture decisions:

- **Project One-pager** – Problem statement, goal, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, risks, and proposed team
- **Project Charter / Overview** – Comprehensive project definition and context
- **Prioritized Backlog** – Work items with acceptance criteria, estimates, and owners
- **Risk Register** – ID, description, impact, likelihood, owner, mitigation plan, and status
- **Definition of Done (DoD)** – Quality standards and acceptance criteria for completed work
- **Release Notes** – Summary of changes, migration steps, and known issues

## Communication & Cadence

OctoAcme maintains structured communication across all projects:

- **Daily Standups** (15 min) – Focus on progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Weekly Project Sync** – Show progress, discuss risks, coordinate across teams
- **Sprint/Milestone Retrospective** – Capture learnings and plan improvements
- **Stakeholder Updates** – Weekly or milestone-based status and announcements
- **Ad-hoc Escalations** – Team → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor for blockers
